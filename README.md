A tiny Python program that writes one line to `/output/entry.txt`: the funniest excuse a robot vacuum can give for missing a spot, at most 200 characters, in the machine's own deadpan voice — smug about its map, its sensors and its reputation. The line was picked by drafting ten candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a real excuse for a missed spot, a laugh that lands rather than a cute dust observation, the machine itself talking, a surprising specific detail over a stock appliance joke, and every word earning its place with the punch at the end — then defending the winner against three fresh challengers in a revision round. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
